Frequently asked

What documentation comes with a shipment?

Dispatch documentation traces each shipment back to the batches and plots it came from. Export shipments additionally require phytosanitary documentation, currently in progress — see the Quality, handling & traceability page for full status.

Can I request a sample before ordering?

Yes — use the quote form below and note the crop and quantity you'd like to sample. A member of the team will follow up on availability and shipping.

Which incoterms and payment terms are supported?

Incoterms and payment terms are confirmed per order. Get in touch through the form below and we'll confirm what's available for your order.
