A farmer training day on the Amuru fields

20 Apr 2026

Draft — for client review before publishing.

Neighbouring smallholders joined a training and demonstration session on the farm this month, covering mechanised planting and Normah's approach to crop rotation and cover cropping.

Sessions like this sit alongside Normah's mechanisation services for smallholders in the district, part of the farm's stated commitment to local hiring and skills transfer.
